## Leader election

Raft splits time into terms. Each term begins with an election. A node that discovers a higher term steps down, which is how a partitioned old leader is retired.

## Log replication

The leader appends entries and replicates them to followers. An entry is committed once a majority has stored it. Only committed entries are applied to the state machine.
